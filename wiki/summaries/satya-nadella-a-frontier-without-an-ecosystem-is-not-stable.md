## Summary

In a widely-shared X thread, [[satya-nadella]], CEO of [[microsoft]], argues that the AI platform shift differs from prior ones: for the first time, digital systems form a "cognitive loop" with people rather than merely enhancing them. He frames every firm's future around two compounding assets — [[human-capital]] (knowledge, judgment, relationships, ingenuity, pattern recognition) and [[token-capital]] (the AI capability the firm builds and owns) — and insists human capital grows *more* valuable as token capital grows, since human agency directs it: "Without human direction, you have compute running in circles."

The real opportunity, he contends, is not picking the best model but building a [[learning-loop]] on top of models where the two capitals compound — a "hill climbing machine" fed by private evals, private reinforcement-learning environments on internal traces, and a queryable knowledge base. This loop becomes the firm's new IP and lets a company swap out a "generalist" model without losing its "company veteran" expertise — the true test of control and sovereignty in the era ahead.

Nadella warns against an AI future where a few models "eat everything they see" and commoditize every industry's knowledge, drawing an analogy to the hollowing-out of industrial economies under globalization. His prescription is to build a *frontier ecosystem*, not just a frontier model, so value flows broadly across every company, industry, and country — one where every organization can own the learning loop that encodes its institutional knowledge. That, he concludes, is the "stable equilibrium we should build together."
