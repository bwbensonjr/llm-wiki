# Memex

Vannevar Bush's 1945 vision (from "As We May Think") of a personal, curated
knowledge store in which the **associative trails** between documents are as
valuable as the documents themselves.

## Notes

Karpathy names the Memex as the spiritual precursor to the
[[llm-wiki|LLM-Wiki]] pattern: Bush's vision was private, actively curated, and
link-centric — closer to the LLM-Wiki idea than to what the web became. The part
Bush could not solve was *who does the maintenance* of those trails; in the
LLM-Wiki pattern the LLM handles it.
