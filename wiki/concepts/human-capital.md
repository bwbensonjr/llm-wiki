In [[satya-nadella]]'s framing of the AI-era firm, human capital is the
knowledge, judgment, relationships, ingenuity, and pattern recognition of a
company's people. He argues it does not lose value as [[token-capital]] grows —
it becomes *more* valuable, because human agency directs AI: "Without human
direction, you have compute running in circles."
