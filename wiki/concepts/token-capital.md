---

[[satya-nadella]]'s term for the AI capability a firm builds and owns — the
counterpart to [[human-capital]]. He argues firms should compound the two
through a [[learning-loop]] (private evals, private RL environments, a queryable
knowledge base) rather than simply adopting the best available model. Token
capital grows under human direction; the loop that grows it becomes the firm's
new IP.
