# Personal Knowledge Management

The practice of accumulating, organizing, and connecting one's own knowledge
over time — notes, sources, and the links between them — so it stays findable
and compounds rather than scattering.

## Notes

The use-case family the [[llm-wiki|LLM-Wiki]] pattern targets: personal
journals and self-tracking, long-running research, reading companions, team
wikis, competitive analysis, and hobby deep-dives. The recurring obstacle is
maintenance burden — humans abandon wikis because upkeep grows faster than
value — which the pattern offloads to the LLM.
