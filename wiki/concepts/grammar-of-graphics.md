# Grammar of Graphics

A coherent system for describing and building statistical graphics by composing
independent components — data, aesthetic mappings, geometric layers, scales,
facets, and themes — rather than drawing charts imperatively.

## Notes

- Underlies both R's [[ggplot2|ggplot2]] and its Python port [[plotnine|Plotnine]].
- Layers inherit data and mappings by default but can override them
  individually.
