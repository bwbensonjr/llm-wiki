# ggplot2

A widely successful R data visualization package implementing the
[[grammar-of-graphics|grammar of graphics]]. The model and syntax that
[[python|Python]]'s [[plotnine|Plotnine]] deliberately mirrors.

## Notes

- Part of the broader R "tidyverse" ecosystem of data-analysis packages.
