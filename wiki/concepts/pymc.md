# PyMC

A [[python|Python]] library for probabilistic programming — building
[[bayesian-inference|Bayesian]] models and fitting them with MCMC and variational
methods.

## Notes

Provides the building blocks for Bayesian models (random variables, deterministic
transforms, `pm.Data` containers) and samplers such as NUTS. Includes
[[gaussian-process|Gaussian process]] support under `pm.gp`, with marginal
likelihoods and covariance kernels like Matérn-5/2.
