# OCaml

A statically-typed functional programming language with a powerful type system.
Adopted at [[jane-street|Jane Street]] — at [[yaron-minsky|Yaron Minsky]]'s
urging — as the firm's primary language, and the basis for its
[[oxcaml|OxCaml]] dialect.

## Notes

- Its expressive [[type-systems|type system]] is the firm's example of a
  lightweight [[formal-methods|formal method]] that pays for itself.
