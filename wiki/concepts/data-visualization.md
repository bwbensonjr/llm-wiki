# Data Visualization

The practice of representing data graphically to support exploration and
communication. Anscombe's Quartet is a classic argument for it: datasets with
identical summary statistics but very different shapes.

## Notes

- The [[grammar-of-graphics|grammar of graphics]] is one influential framework
  for building visualizations, realized in [[ggplot2|ggplot2]] and
  [[plotnine|Plotnine]].
