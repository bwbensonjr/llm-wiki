# Obsidian

A local-first Markdown note editor that resolves `[[wikilinks]]` by filename and
visualizes a vault as a graph.

## Notes

In the [[llm-wiki|LLM-Wiki]] pattern, Obsidian is the human's read-side
"IDE": the LLM edits pages from the conversation while the curator browses the
results in real time — following links, checking the graph view, reading updated
pages. The graph view surfaces hubs and orphans; community plugins (Web Clipper,
Marp, Dataview) extend ingestion and output. This wiki is authored as plain
Markdown with YAML front-matter specifically so it opens directly in Obsidian.
