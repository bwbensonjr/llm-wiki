In [[satya-nadella]]'s framing, the learning loop is the system a firm builds on
top of models where [[human-capital]] and [[token-capital]] compound — a "hill
climbing machine." It is fed by private evals (measuring whether a model
improves against business outcomes, not external benchmarks), private
reinforcement-learning environments trained on internal traces, and a queryable
knowledge base of institutional memory. The loop becomes the firm's new IP and
lets it swap out a "generalist" model without losing its "company veteran"
expertise — Nadella's test of control and sovereignty.
