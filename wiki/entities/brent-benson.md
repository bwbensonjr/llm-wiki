# Brent W. Benson Jr.

Software engineer and the curator of this wiki. Author of
[[libscheme-scheme-as-a-c-library|libscheme]], a library that makes
[[scheme|Scheme]] available as an embeddable C library.

## Notes

At the time of the libscheme paper (1994) he was a senior software engineer in
the compiler group at Harris Computer Systems, having received a BA in
Mathematics from the University of Rochester (1990) and completed the work for
an MS in Computer Science at the University of New Hampshire (1992). libscheme
was later used as the starting point for [[matthew-flatt|Matthew Flatt]]'s
MzScheme, the ancestor of [[racket|Racket]] — a lineage documented in
[[porting-racket-to-chez-scheme|Porting Racket to Chez Scheme]], which cites
libscheme as "Benson 1994."
