# Vikram Adve

A professor of computer science at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign
and the faculty originator of the [[llvm]] project (the "first author" of the
CACM retrospective). His NSF CAREER grant funded LLVM's early development.

## Notes

Co-author of "The LLVM Compiler Infrastructure." Starting a new research program
at UIUC in 2000, he set out to design flexible dynamic-compilation techniques for
arbitrary programming languages; his CAREER proposal to the NSF Next Generation
Software program (Grant EIA-0093426), submitted July 2000, funded most of both
authors' early work on LLVM. His later LLVM-based research includes virtual
instruction set architectures, compiler-based memory safety for C, and the HPVM
heterogeneous-compilation project.
