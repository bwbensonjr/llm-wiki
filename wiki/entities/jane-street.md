# Jane Street

A proprietary trading firm known in the programming-languages world for its
heavy use of [[ocaml|OCaml]] and its investment in language tooling. The author of
[[oxcaml|OxCaml]] and a prolific publisher of engineering writing.

## Notes

- Long held that full [[formal-methods|formal methods]] were not worth the cost
  for most software; reversed that stance in 2026 as
  [[agentic-coding|agentic coding]] changed the cost/benefit calculus, and is
  now building a formal methods team.
