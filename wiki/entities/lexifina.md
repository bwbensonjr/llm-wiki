# Lexifina

Lexifina develops global-law, a CC0 repository of versioned legislation and
case law, and describes using AI and the [[lean-proof-assistant|Lean proof assistant]]
for [[legislation-formalization|legislation formalization]]. Its
US tax-code work combines drafting checks, an executable income-tax model,
proofs, and searches for interactions among rules.
