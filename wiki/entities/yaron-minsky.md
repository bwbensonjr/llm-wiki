# Yaron Minsky

A technologist at [[jane-street|Jane Street]], which he joined in 2002; credited
with convincing the firm to adopt [[ocaml|OCaml]]. A frequent voice on programming
languages, tooling, and software quality.

## Notes

- Authored the 2026 announcement that Jane Street is embracing
  [[formal-methods|formal methods]], reversing a long-held skeptical position.
