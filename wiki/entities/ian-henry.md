# Ian Henry

Programmer and writer at [ianthehenry.com](https://ianthehenry.com/); author of
the free book *Janet for Mortals* (janet.guide).

## Notes

An advocate for [[janet|Janet]], which he describes as his go-to language for
side projects. His writing makes the practical case for the language and for
Lisp-style metaprogramming.
