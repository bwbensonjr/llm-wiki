# John Reppy

A professor of computer science at the University of Chicago, working on
programming-language design and implementation, concurrency, and compilers. He
is known for Concurrent ML, contributions to [[standard-ml|Standard ML]] of New
Jersey, and leadership of the Manticore parallel-functional-language project.

## Notes

Co-author (with Kavon Farvardin) of "Compiling with Continuations and LLVM,"
which adds a heap-allocated first-class continuation model to an [[llvm]]
backend for the Manticore/PML compiler.
