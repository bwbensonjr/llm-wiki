# Matthias Felleisen

A Trustee Professor in the College of Computer Science at Northeastern
University, Boston, MA. A principal architect of [[racket]] and a long-standing
researcher in programming languages, contracts, and pedagogic language design.

## Notes

Lead author of "A Programmable Programming Language," the manifesto for
[[language-oriented-programming]]. His co-authors on the paper are Robert Bruce
Findler (Northwestern), Matthew Flatt (Utah), Shriram Krishnamurthi (Brown), Eli
Barzilay (Microsoft Research), Jay McCarthy (UMass Lowell), and Sam
Tobin-Hochstadt (Indiana) — the core of the Racket research group. His earlier
work cited in the paper includes the expressive-power theory of programming
languages, higher-order contracts, and the Blame Theorem (with Tobin-Hochstadt).
