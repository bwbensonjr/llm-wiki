CEO of [[microsoft]]. In a 2026 X thread he argued that firms must build both
[[human-capital]] and [[token-capital]], compounding them through a
[[learning-loop]] rather than chasing the best individual model, and called for
a "frontier ecosystem" so AI value flows broadly rather than concentrating in a
few models.
