# Chris Lattner

A compiler and programming-language engineer who, as a UIUC Ph.D. student
beginning Fall 2000, designed and implemented the core of [[llvm]] (the "second
author" of the CACM retrospective). He later drove LLVM's commercial adoption at
Apple and created several major derivative systems.

## Notes

Co-author of "The LLVM Compiler Infrastructure." He built the initial LLVM
skeleton over the winter break after Fall 2000, and its core design features —
modular library structure, a clean IR with equivalent in-memory/textual/binary
forms, and a Separation-of-Concerns pass pipeline — remain visible in LLVM today.
At Apple he developed Clang (an open-source GCC alternative for C/C++/Objective-C)
and the Swift programming language, and he later led the MLIR project — all
designed around LLVM.
